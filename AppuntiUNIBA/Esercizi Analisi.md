$\mathcal{B}=\left( v_{1},\dots,v_{n} \right)$ indipendenti, base di $V$. $w_{1},\dots,w_{p}$, $p>n$ vettori di $V$. Allora $w_{1},\dots,w_{p}$ sono dipendenti,
Se non lo fossero: $$
\alpha_{1}w_{1}+\dots+\alpha_{p}w_{p}=0
$$
$w_{1}=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}\beta_{i}v_{i}$. Allora $\alpha_{2}w_{2}+\dots+\alpha_{p}w_{p}=-\alpha_{1}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}\beta_{i}v_{i}$.
Quindi: $$
\alpha_{2}w_{2}+\dots+\alpha_{p}w_{p}=\alpha_{1}\beta_{1}v_{1}+\dots\alpha_{1}\beta_{n}v_{n}.
$$
$$
v_{n}=\frac{\alpha_{2}w_{2}+\dots+\alpha_{p}w_{p}}{\alpha_{1}\beta_{n}}.
$$
